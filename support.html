Frequently asked questions

How do I back up my jobs, or move them to a new phone?

Your jobs are stored only on your phone, so back them up regularly.

  • Back up: Settings → Export backup (JSON). This makes one file with every job, survey and peg test. Save it to Files, email it to yourself or AirDrop it.
  • Restore: Settings → Import backup (JSON) and pick the file. Jobs in the file are added; a job that is already on the phone is replaced by the copy in the file.
  • New phone: export on the old phone, send the file to the new phone, install the app, then import.
  • Plan sheet PDFs are stored separately on the device. Keep your original plan files so you can attach them again on a new phone.

How does the 7-day free trial work?
  • The trial starts only when you tap START 7-DAY FREE TRIAL (on any Pro feature, or Settings → Plan). No account and no card are needed.
  • Every Pro feature is unlocked for 7 days. There is one trial per device.
  • When the trial ends the app goes back to Free. Nothing is deleted: Pro surveys you made stay readable, and single-wire Level Runs and Site Levels stay fully editable.
How are closures checked?
  • Arithmetic check on every book: ΣBS − ΣFS = last elevation − first elevation (side shots are not included).
  • Misclosure = computed elevation of the closing benchmark − its known elevation. A Level Loop closes on its starting benchmark; a Level Run closes on a different known benchmark.
  • Allowable misclosure follows the FGCS standards: k × √M, where M is the total sight distance in miles (k = 0.050 ft for third order, the default; 2nd order class II 0.0333, class I 0.025; 1st order class II 0.0166, class I 0.0125). The app shows a clear pass or fail.
  • If Use distance is off, there is no distance to work out the allowable misclosure, so the misclosure is shown and adjustment is by setups.
  • Pro can adjust the loop evenly by setup or in proportion to distance.
What units does the app use?

US survey feet, in decimal feet. Readings are entered and shown to 0.001 ft; cut and fill are shown to 0.01 ft. Curvature and refraction are not applied.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. After the first start the app works fully offline, including reports, plan sheets and the built-in manual.

My jobs disappeared from the web app.

Safari can clear data for websites that have not been used for a while. Add the web app to your Home Screen, turn on Keep data on this device in Settings, and export backups regularly. Import your latest backup to get your jobs back.
