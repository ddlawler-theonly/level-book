Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

The short version

  • BlueWorks Level Book works offline. Your jobs, readings, plan sheets and settings stay on your device.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The app has no third-party SDKs.
  • Nothing leaves your device unless you send it yourself: a report you share, a backup you export, or feedback you send.

Who we are

BlueWorks Level Book (“the app”) is published by BlueWorks Field Pro (Derek Lawler) (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the iPhone app and the web app at ddlawler-theonly.github.io/level-book.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter is stored only on your device, in the app’s own storage:

  • Jobs and surveys – job details, benchmarks, rod readings, distances, peg tests, stake-out records and your settings are kept in the app’s local storage (localStorage).
  • Plan sheet PDFs – plan files you attach, and the pins you place on them, are kept in the app’s on-device database (IndexedDB). Plan files are never uploaded.
  • Free trial record – the date your 7-day Pro trial started is kept on the device (in local storage and IndexedDB) so the trial can be used once per device. It is not sent anywhere.
  • Automatic backup (iPhone app) – the iPhone app also keeps a backup copy of your jobs on the device, in the app’s settings storage and as LevelBook-autobackup.json in the app’s folder in the Files app.

We cannot see, access or recover this data. If your device itself is backed up (for example by iCloud Backup or a computer backup), the app’s data may be included in that backup under your own device settings.

What we do not collect

  • No account, sign-in, name or email is needed to use the app.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising services.
  • No location data. Elevations and plan pins are typed or tapped by you.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no data is sold or shared with data brokers.

Network use

The app does not contact any server while you work. The web app is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you first open it, or when it checks for an update, your browser downloads the app’s files from GitHub, which may record standard server information such as your IP address as part of serving the page (see the GitHub Privacy Statement). After that the app runs from your device, including offline.

Reports, exports and sharing

PDF reports, CSV and text exports, and backup files are created on your device. They go only where you send them – for example Mail, Messages, AirDrop or the Files app – using your device’s share sheet. What happens to a file after you send it is controlled by the app or person you sent it to.

Feedback

The Send feedback form does not send anything by itself. When you tap SEND FEEDBACK, the app prepares a message and opens your device’s share sheet or your email app, already addressed to the developer. The message contains:

  • what you chose and typed: the star rating, the category and your notes;
  • information added so we can reproduce a problem: the app version, your device and browser description (user-agent), screen size, the theme you use, and the name of the screen, job and survey you were on;
  • a PDF report of the current survey, only if you tick Attach current survey report.

You can read and edit the message before sending it, and nothing is sent unless you send it. If you send it, we receive it by email and use it only to reply to you and to improve the app.

Purchases

Pro purchases and subscriptions in the iPhone app are handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; the app never sees your card or payment details. The app only learns whether Pro is active on your device. Apple’s handling of your purchase is covered by Apple’s own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Delete a job or plan sheet in the app to remove it. Deleting the iPhone app, or clearing the website data for the web app, removes everything the app stored on that device. Export a backup first if you want to keep your work.

Children

The app is a professional surveying tool. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above.
